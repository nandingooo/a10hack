# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

User selected static HTML/CSS/JavaScript served by the Python controller.

## Users

Hackathon team members launching supervised coding agents and judges assessing
traffic, decisions, evidence, and termination in a live demonstration.

## Product Purpose

TrustAgent labels context by integrity and confidentiality, preserves its
provenance, and evaluates consequential tool actions at a central gateway.

## Operating Context

Local development machine with Docker Desktop. A managed session has a sandbox,
an egress gateway, policy and provenance state, and an operator control plane.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Show actual attempts, execution results, exposure, accumulated labels and source
evidence. STOP must report observed process termination. Distinguish inference
from observation and scripted rehearsal from language-model execution. Avoid
claims of exact causal tracking or universal prompt-injection protection.

## Brand Commitments

TrustAgent name. Direct engineering language and honest explanations.

## Evidence on Hand

Python tests, local hook/HTTP integration, and a scripted managed rehearsal.
Docker isolation and live UI verification are in progress; do not imply they are
complete until their acceptance evidence is recorded.
